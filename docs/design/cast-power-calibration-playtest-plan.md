# Cast Power Calibration Playtest

The current cast system maps a fixed gyroscope range (`1.5–7 rad/s`) to
`0–100%` power. These values are temporary and may not suit every player or
phone. This playtest will check whether personal calibration improves control.

After two practice casts, each participant will make five light casts, five
normal casts, and five strong but comfortable casts. The game will record the
filtered peak angular velocity of each movement. The median light value will
become the player's minimum peak, and the median strong value will become their
maximum peak. Normal casts will show whether the middle of the range feels
natural. Calibration will be repeated if the strong result is not higher than
the light result.

Players will then compare the fixed profile with their personal profile. In
each mode, they will attempt near, middle, and far casts. We will record cast
power, landing position, missed inputs, and repeated 100% casts. Players will
also rate control and comfort.

The results will show whether personal calibration improves accuracy and
reduces uncomfortable movement. The shared profile will remain as a fallback;
successful personal calibration will be saved separately for future sessions.
